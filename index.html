What We Build

An AI Workflow Is a System, Not a Prompt

Buying a ChatGPT seat won’t give your team SE-level velocity. The leverage comes from workflows—connected to your product, your docs, and your CRM—that take a real input and produce a usable output every time.

I approach every engagement the way an engineer approaches a system: find the bottleneck, design the workflow, wire it into your tools, and measure the output. The code motif isn’t decoration—it’s how these workflows actually get built.

demo-prep-workflow.js

const demoPrep = async (prospect) => {

inputs: [

"Discovery call transcript",

"CRM context + tech stack",

"Your product docs"

],

ai.generate({

painPoints: "Mapped to features",

demoFlow: "Tailored narrative",

objections: "Likely + responses"

}),

output: "Demo brief in your inbox"

};

// Real inputs in, usable output out. Every time.
