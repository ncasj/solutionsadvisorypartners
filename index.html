The Standard

Every Pilot Ends With a Scorecard, Not a Vibe

Before I build anything, we write down what success means in numbers: hours returned, turnaround time, error rate. The pilot runs on your real data for real work. Then we read the scorecard together.

If it passes, we scale it. If it doesn’t, you stop—having spent a fraction of what an agency retainer costs, and knowing exactly why. Either outcome beats another quarter of guessing.
