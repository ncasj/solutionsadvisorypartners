The Implementation Handoff Checklist Every SE Team Needs

5 min read

The handoff from pre-sales to post-sales is one of the highest-risk moments in the customer lifecycle. When it goes well, implementation starts smoothly and the customer feels confident. When it doesn't, the implementation team inherits broken promises, undocumented requirements, and a customer who's already losing trust.

A structured handoff checklist eliminates the guesswork. It ensures that every piece of critical information captured during the sales process—technical requirements, integration decisions, success criteria, stakeholder context, and scope boundaries—transfers cleanly to the team responsible for delivery.

Most SE teams know this matters. Few have formalized it. The common items that should transfer include: discovery notes, technical requirements documentation, POC results and learnings, agreed-upon success criteria, integration architecture decisions, key stakeholder map, scope boundaries and known exclusions, and timeline commitments.
