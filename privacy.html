Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Proofworks (“we,” “I”) operates this website. This policy explains what data is collected when you visit, how it’s used, and the choices you have. The short version: analytics only run if you consent, form data goes only toward replying to you, and nothing is sold to anyone.

What We Collect

1. Analytics Data (Consent Required)

If—and only if—you accept the analytics prompt, we use Mixpanel (including session recording) and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, buttons clicked, assessment completions, and approximate location derived from your IP. If you decline, no analytics scripts load and no tracking occurs.

2. Contact & Assessment Form Data

When you submit the contact form or request assessment results by email, the information you provide (name, email, company, role, team size, and your message or score) is sent to us via EmailJS so we can respond. It is used solely to reply to your inquiry and, where you’ve engaged with the assessment, to send your results.

Cookies & Local Storage

The site stores your analytics consent choice in your browser’s local storage so we don’t re-ask on every visit. If you consent to analytics, Mixpanel sets cookies/local-storage entries to distinguish visitors. No advertising cookies are used.

How to Opt Out

Decline the consent prompt, or clear your browser’s local storage for this site to be asked again. You can also email us (below) to request deletion of anything you’ve submitted.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export the personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us—there’s no formal process, a plain-language request is enough. We do not sell personal information.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are kept in email for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any resulting engagement. Analytics data is retained under Mixpanel’s standard retention settings. You can request deletion of either at any time.

Third-Party Services

  • Mixpanel — analytics & session recording, loaded only after consent (privacy policy)
  • Google Analytics — analytics, loaded only after consent (privacy policy)
  • EmailJS — delivers form submissions to our inbox (privacy policy)
  • Calendly — scheduling, if you choose to book a call (privacy policy)
  • Google Fonts — typefaces served from Google’s CDN
  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the “last updated” date above will change with it. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
