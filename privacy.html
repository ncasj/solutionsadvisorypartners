Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 5, 2026

Overview

Solutions Advisory Partners ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to transparency about how we collect and use data on our website solutionsadvisory.co. This policy explains what information we gather, why, and your choices regarding that data.

What We Collect

1. Analytics Data (Consent Required)

When you accept analytics cookies via our consent banner, we use the following services:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — collects anonymized page views, referral sources, device type, and general geographic region. We use this to understand aggregate traffic patterns.
  • Mixpanel — collects behavioral analytics including page views, click interactions, heatmap data, and session recordings. Session recordings capture mouse movements and page interactions but do not record passwords or payment fields. We use this to improve user experience.

These services are only loaded after you click "Accept" on our consent banner. If you decline or ignore the banner, no analytics scripts run and no tracking cookies are placed.

2. Contact Form Data

When you submit our contact form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your work email address
  • The content of your message

This data is sent to us via EmailJS and delivered to our email inbox. We do not store form submissions in a database. If you have accepted analytics, your email address may also be associated with your Mixpanel profile for the purpose of connecting your inquiry to your site activity.

How to Opt Out

  • Before consenting: Simply click "Decline" on the cookie banner. No analytics will load.
  • After consenting: Clear your browser's localStorage for this site (remove the sap_analytics_consent key), then reload. The consent banner will reappear.
  • Browser-level: You can also use browser extensions like uBlock Origin or disable JavaScript to prevent all tracking.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are located in the EU/EEA or California, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (see "How to Opt Out" above)
  • Object to data processing for direct marketing

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@solutionsadvisorypartners.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained according to each provider's default retention policies (Google Analytics: 14 months; Mixpanel: 5 years or per plan limits). Contact form data is retained in our email inbox indefinitely unless you request deletion.

Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics (analytics)
  • Mixpanel (analytics & session recording)
  • EmailJS (contact form delivery)
  • Netlify (hosting)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
