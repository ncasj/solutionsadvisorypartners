What an Engagement Looks Like

Illustrative examples of AI workflows in action for lean teams. Problem → Workflow → What Changes.

From Founder-Led Demos to AI-Prepped Demos

API integration platform · ~25 people · technical product, no SE on staff · illustrative

Demo & POC Acceleration

Problem

The two founders ran every technical demo themselves. AEs gave the same generic product tour to everyone, and it didn’t connect to the buyer’s problem. Technical deals stalled waiting for a founder to free up.

Workflow

Built a demo-prep workflow grounded in their product docs and ICP. For each opportunity it drafts a buyer-specific demo narrative, a talk track, and a likely-objection list—reviewed by the AE before the call. Tuned to three core buyer types.

What Changes

  • AEs run tailored demos without a founder in the room
  • Every demo is mapped to the buyer’s problem
  • Founders get most of their demo time back
  • Deals stop stalling on technical availability

Engagement: Demo & POC Acceleration · Built: Demo-prep workflow wired to product docs, buyer-type templates, objection library, AE review checklist

Solution Design: Days to Minutes

Data analytics platform · ~40 people · selling into security-conscious buyers · illustrative

Solution Design & Onboarding

Problem

Every enterprise deal came with a security questionnaire or RFP. They ate days of the founder’s and lead engineer’s time, answers were copied inconsistently from old emails, and deals sat idle waiting on them.

Workflow

Built a security & RFP responder grounded in a single source of truth: their security posture, policies, and approved past answers. It drafts responses to each questionnaire in minutes; the team reviews and approves rather than writing from scratch.

What Changes

  • First drafts in minutes instead of days
  • Answers are consistent and on-policy
  • Deals stop stalling on security review
  • Founder and lead engineer reclaim hours per deal

Engagement: Solution Design & Onboarding Workflows · Built: Security source-of-truth knowledge base, questionnaire/RFP responder, human review & approval step

Onboarding Handoffs That Actually Transfer

Workflow automation platform · ~45 people · founder-led sales handing to a small CS team · illustrative

Solution Design & Onboarding

Problem

What got promised in the sale lived in the founder’s head. The CS team kicked off blind, customers had to repeat themselves, and onboarding dragged—setting a bad tone right when new accounts were most fragile.

Workflow

Built an onboarding & handoff workflow that pulls from call notes, the CRM, and the deal’s commitments to generate a structured handoff summary—goals, integrations, success criteria, and open risks—the moment a deal closes.

What Changes

  • CS starts with full context, not a blank page
  • Customers don’t have to repeat themselves
  • What was sold is what gets delivered
  • New accounts start strong, not behind

Engagement: Solution Design & Onboarding Workflows · Built: Handoff-summary workflow wired to CRM & call notes, structured summary template, CS kickoff checklist

FAQ

Common Questions

How do you determine final pricing?

Every engagement starts with a scoping call where we assess your current state, team size, and goals. Pricing is based on scope and complexity—not hourly rates.

Can I combine multiple packages?

Yes. Many teams start with the AI Readiness & Workflow Audit, then move into a specific workflow build or an ongoing Fractional SE Partner engagement. We’ll recommend the right sequence.

What’s the minimum commitment for the Fractional SE Partner?

We recommend a minimum 3-month engagement to see meaningful results. Month-to-month after that—no long-term lock-in.

Do we need a Solutions Engineer already on staff?

No—that’s the point. These workflows are designed for lean teams that don’t have an SE function yet. If you do have one, the same workflows simply give them more leverage.

What if we’re not sure which package is right?

That’s what the scoping call is for. We’ll assess your situation and recommend the right starting point—no obligation. Most teams start with the Audit.

Isn’t this just buying everyone a ChatGPT subscription?

No. A chat tool gives you a blank box; we build workflows connected to your product docs, your CRM, and your sales process that take a real input and produce a usable, reviewable output every time—with humans in the loop where it matters.

Do we need engineering resources to build these workflows?

Not for the Build + Implement approach—we handle the engineering. The Advisory approach is designed for teams that have a dev team or vendor they’d direct. If you’re unsure, the scoping call will help us recommend the right fit.
