How an Engagement Works

A structured path from first call to a fully operational Revenue Delivery Architecture.

1

Discovery Call

30 min

Learn about your GTM motion, team structure, and where deals are stalling.

Deliverable: Fit assessment.

2

Technical GTM Audit

1–2 weeks

Deep-dive diagnostic into your technical sales process, team, and handoff systems.

Deliverable: Gap analysis & recommendations.

3

System Design

2–3 weeks

Architect the solution tailored to your stage, market, and team.

Deliverable: RDA blueprint & roadmap.

4

Build & Implement

3–6 weeks

Deploy frameworks, train the team, and stand up operational systems.

Deliverable: Playbooks, templates, systems.

Optimize & Scale

Deliverable: KPI dashboards, coaching.
