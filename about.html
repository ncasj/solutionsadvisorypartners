Our Approach

What a Solutions Engineer Does, Rebuilt as AI Workflows.

A great Solutions Engineer isn’t magic—they’re a repeatable process. They prep tailored demos, answer technical and security questions fast, keep POCs on track, and hand off cleanly to delivery. The reason it feels magic is that most teams have never written it down, let alone systematized it.

For a lean team, that’s the opening. A large share of SE work is structured, document-heavy, and pattern-driven—exactly what modern AI is good at when it’s wired into your real product docs, security posture, and CRM. The point isn’t to replace judgment; it’s to take the repeatable 80% off your plate so your people spend their time where it actually moves a deal.

So instead of hiring an SE and hoping they build all this from scratch, we map the technical moments in your deal cycle and build a workflow for each one—tuned to how you sell.

It starts before the demo. A demo-prep workflow pulls the prospect’s context—their industry, stack, and stated problem—and drafts a tailored demo narrative, talk track, and likely-objection list, so your AE walks in sounding like they brought an SE. No more generic product tours.

Then comes solution design—the stage where the buyer’s technical team pressure-tests the fit, and security reviews and RFP questionnaires start piling up. A workflow grounded in your real security posture and past answers drafts those responses in minutes instead of days, so your team reviews and approves instead of starting from a blank spreadsheet at 11pm.

During the POC, a tracking workflow keeps success criteria, open items, and stakeholder updates in one place, so evaluations don’t quietly expand and stall. And at the finish line, an onboarding-and-handoff workflow turns everything that was promised in the sale into a clean summary for whoever delivers it—so what was sold is what gets delivered.

None of this is a generic chatbot bolted onto your site. It’s a set of workflows designed around your product and your motion—built by someone who has run these plays as a Solutions Engineering leader, delivered either as advisory guidance or a full build-and-implement engagement.

The goal is simple: give your team the velocity of a Solutions Engineer—before you hire one.
